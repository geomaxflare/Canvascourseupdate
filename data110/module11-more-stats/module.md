# Module 11: More Statistics

## Introduction

In Module 5, you learned to describe data and to estimate a population value from a sample with a confidence interval. In Module 6, you fit regression models. This module answers the question that comes next in almost every analysis: **is the difference or relationship I see in my data real, or could it have happened by chance?**

You will learn the logic of **hypothesis testing** and how to read a **p-value**, then apply the three tests analysts use most: the **t-test** for comparing averages, the **chi-square test** for comparing categories, and **correlation** for measuring how strongly two numerical variables move together. These methods are part of the CompTIA Data+ (DA0-002) exam objectives, and they are how analysts back up recommendations such as "the new website layout increased sales" with evidence instead of opinion.

## Objectives

- Write null and alternative hypotheses for a business or research question.
- Interpret a p-value and a significance level, and explain Type I and Type II errors.
- Choose an appropriate statistical test based on the type of data and the question asked.
- Run and interpret one-sample, two-sample, and paired t-tests in R or Excel.
- Run and interpret chi-square goodness of fit and independence tests in R or Excel.
- Distinguish correlation from causation, and statistical significance from practical importance.

## Why This Matters

Data analysts are regularly asked to decide whether a change made a difference: a new marketing campaign, a different store layout, a new tutoring program. Without a test, it is easy to mistake random variation for a real effect, and organizations make costly decisions based on noise. The skills in this module let you state how confident you are in a finding, which is what turns an observation into a recommendation.
