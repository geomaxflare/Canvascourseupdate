# Module 12: AI Fluency for Data Analytics

## Introduction

AI assistants such as Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude can now write SQL, explain R errors, suggest charts, and draft reports. Used well, they make an analyst faster and more thorough. Used carelessly, they produce confident, wrong answers, expose private data, and hide mistakes inside polished output.

This module builds **AI fluency**: the ability to work with AI effectively, efficiently, ethically, and safely. It is organized around the **4D AI Fluency Framework** (Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence), and you will apply it to problems you have already solved in this course: cleaning the census data in SQL, running hypothesis tests in R, and interpreting regression output. Because you already know the right answers to these problems, you will be able to see clearly where AI helps, where it makes mistakes, and how to catch them.

## Objectives

- Explain how generative AI and large language models work, and describe their strengths, limitations, and typical errors.
- Decide which parts of an analytics task to delegate to AI and which to keep for yourself (Delegation).
- Write clear, structured prompts and refine results through iteration and prompt chaining (Description).
- Verify AI output for accuracy, completeness, and reasoning, including code and statistical results (Discernment).
- Use AI responsibly: protect sensitive data, follow policy, check for bias, and disclose AI use (Diligence).
- Apply AI across the data analysis process, from asking the question to communicating results.

## Why This Matters

Employers increasingly expect entry-level analysts to use AI tools, and they also expect them to know when *not* to trust those tools. An analyst who pastes customer records into a public chatbot, or reports a number an AI invented, can cause real harm. This module gives you a repeatable way to get value from AI while staying accountable for the result, and it prepares you to earn short, recognized AI credentials that you can list on your resume.
